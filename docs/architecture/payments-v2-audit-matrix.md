## 2. Identified Invariants & Rules

1. **Zero-Custody Rail**: GymDeck never holds, escrows, or pools funds. Payments route directly from member to owner bank accounts.
2. **Authoritative Amounts**: Amounts are strictly computed server-side in integer minor units (paise: 1 INR = 100 paise).
3. **No Automatic Client Verification**: A client callback, UPI return, or member-submitted UTR is NEVER proof of settlement. Payments remain `SUBMITTED` / `PENDING_VERIFICATION` until verified by an authorized operator.
4. **Row-Level Concurrency Protection**: Verification transactions use `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` locks on `payment_attempts` and `invoices` to prevent double-crediting.
5. **Strict Tenant Isolation**: `gym_id` is derived strictly from the authenticated JWT session (`req.user.gymId`). Client-supplied `gym_id` parameters are ignored.
6. **Encrypted Credentials**: Bank account numbers are encrypted at rest via AES-256 (`account_number_enc`). Only masked `last4` is exposed in UI and APIs.
