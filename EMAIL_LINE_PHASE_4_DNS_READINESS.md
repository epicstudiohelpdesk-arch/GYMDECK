## 1. Domain Architecture & Collision Isolation

The root domain `epic-studio.in` currently hosts operational Google Workspace email infrastructure:
- **Root MX:** Google Workspace (`aspmx.l.google.com`, priority 1; `alt1.aspmx.l.google.com`, priority 5, etc.)
- **Root SPF:** `v=spf1 include:dc-aa8e722993._spfm.epic-studio.in ~all` (resolves to Google Workspace `_spf.google.com`)
- **Root DMARC:** `v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; adkim=r; aspf=r; rua=mailto:dmarc_rua@onsecureserver.net;`

### Architectural Decision: Dedicated Subdomain (`mail.epic-studio.in`)
To ensure that GymDeck Email Line:
1. **Never collides** with or alters Google Workspace MX records.
2. **Never overwrites or invalidates** the existing SPF record.
3. Preserves full DMARC compliance under the organizational domain `epic-studio.in`.

Postal has been provisioned exclusively for `mail.epic-studio.in`. Proposed sender identity:
`GymDeck <notifications@mail.epic-studio.in>`
